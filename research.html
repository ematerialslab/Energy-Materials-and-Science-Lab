Research

Research interests and fields

Three connected directions that cover the life of a battery: making today's lithium-ion cells better, building the solid-state cells that come next, and recovering materials when batteries reach end of life.

01

Advanced Li-ion batteries

We develop electrode materials and cell designs that push lithium-ion batteries toward higher capacity, faster charging and longer life without compromising safety. Our work covers Ni-rich layered oxide cathodes, high-capacity anodes and electrode engineering for practical cells.

Focus areas
  • High capacity and energy density
  • Fast charging and discharging
  • Long cycle performance
  • High safety
02

All-solid-state battery systems

All-solid-state batteries replace the flammable liquid electrolyte with a solid ion conductor. We study sulfide and halide solid electrolytes, high-capacity anodes such as lithium metal and silicon, and solvent-free dry electrodes that keep solid–solid contact intact during cycling.

Focus areas
  • High-capacity electrode materials: Li metal and Si-based materials
  • High ionic conductivity
  • High safety
03

Battery recycling

We recover valuable metals from spent lithium-ion batteries and turn them back into functional materials, including regenerated cathodes and catalysts. The goal is a closed loop that lowers both cost and environmental impact.

Focus areas
  • Used battery recycling
  • Valuable metal recovery
  • Reproduction of cathode materials
  • Production of catalyst materials
