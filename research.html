Research

Research areas

Four connected directions across the life of a battery, from better cells today to the materials loop that brings them back.

01Li-ion02Solid-state03Na-ion04Upcycling
01

Advanced Li-ion batteries

Higher capacity, faster charging and longer life for lithium-ion cells, without giving up safety.

Ni-rich layered oxide cathodesCo-precipitation and solid-state synthesis of NCM622 and NCM811 with controlled composition and morphology.
High-capacity silicon–carbon anodesCarbon-coated silicon composites that buffer volume change while keeping high capacity.
Fast charging and long cycle lifeElectrode and interface design for high rate capability and stable cycling.
02

All-solid-state battery systems

Replacing flammable liquid electrolytes with solid ion conductors for safer, higher-energy cells.

Dry-processed composite cathodesSolvent-free NCM811 / sulfide electrolyte / PTFE films with high areal loading.
Sulfide and halide solid electrolytesArgyrodite Li₆PS₅Cl and niobium oxychloride halides with high ionic conductivity.
Lithium-metal and silicon anodesCarbon-coated Li metal and dry Si–C films for stable cycling at practical stack pressure.

03

Na-ion batteries

Low-cost, sustainable energy storage built on abundant sodium.

Layered oxide (NFM) cathodesSodium layered oxides with stable cycling and high capacity.
Biomass-derived hard carbon anodesHard carbons from agricultural and marine biomass with high initial Coulombic efficiency.
Dry electrode processingSolvent-free electrodes with conductive additives for practical Na-ion cells.
04

Battery upcycling

Recovering valuable metals from spent batteries and turning them back into higher-value materials.

Metal recoveryRecovery of Li, Ni, Co and Mn from black mass of spent lithium-ion batteries.
Regenerated NCM cathodesRe-synthesis of cathode materials from recovered metals, closing the loop to new batteries.
Catalysts from recovered metalsConverting recovered transition metals into electrocatalysts.
