Energy Materials for a Sustainable Future.

Design · Synthesize · Understand · Innovate. We develop materials for advanced lithium-ion and all-solid-state batteries, and recover them from spent cells.

Our Direction

01 Vision

Leading energy materials research for safe, high-energy batteries.

We develop electrode materials, solid electrolytes and cell designs that make batteries safer, longer-lasting and more energy-dense.

02 Mission

Innovating across the full battery life cycle, from material to recycled resource.

From material design and synthesis to electrochemical analysis and recycling of spent batteries, we work on every stage of a battery's life.

02

Research Areas

Materials, by design.

We design, synthesize and understand the particles inside a battery, and how they change over thousands of cycles.

Area 01

Advanced Li-ion batteries

Higher capacity, faster charging
  • High capacity and energy density
  • Fast charging and discharging
  • Long cycle performance
  • High safety
Area 02

All-solid-state battery systems

Safer cells with solid electrolytes
  • Li metal and Si-based electrodes
  • High ionic conductivity
  • High safety

Area 03

Na-ion batteries

Low-cost, abundant sodium
  • Layered oxide (NFM) cathodes
  • Biomass-derived hard carbon anodes
  • High initial Coulombic efficiency
  • Sustainable, low-cost storage
Area 04

Battery upcycling

Closing the materials loop
  • Metal recovery from spent batteries
  • Regenerated NCM cathodes
  • Catalysts from recovered metals
  • Closed-loop battery materials
