Energy Materials Lab

About the lab

Materials science for safer, longer-lasting energy storage

Led by Prof. Hyungjung Park in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering, our group works across the full battery chain: synthesizing active materials, engineering electrodes, building and testing cells, and recovering valuable metals at end of life.

We combine materials chemistry with electrochemical analysis to understand why materials succeed or fail, and use that understanding to build batteries with higher energy density, faster charging and better safety.
